About BTS

BTS is a South Korean music group that debuted in 2013 under Big Hit Entertainment, now known as BIGHIT MUSIC. The group consists of seven members: RM, Jin, SUGA, j-hope, Jimin, V, and Jung Kook. The name BTS comes from the Korean name Bangtan Sonyeondan, which translates to “Bulletproof Boy Scouts.” BTS has also used the meaning “Beyond the Scene” to represent their growth and continued development. Over the years, BTS has become one of the most internationally recognized groups in K-pop.

🎶 BTS and Their Music

One of the things that makes BTS interesting is the variety of music they have released throughout their career. Their music has explored genres such as hip-hop, pop, R&B, rock, and electronic music. Their earlier releases often focused on themes related to school, youth, and the challenges young people experience. As their career developed, their music explored subjects such as identity, relationships, self-love, dreams, loneliness, and personal growth.

🌎 BTS's Global Legacy

BTS has had a major impact on the international popularity of K-pop. They have performed for audiences around the world and have introduced many listeners to Korean music and culture. Their success has also helped demonstrate that music can connect people across different languages and cultures.
